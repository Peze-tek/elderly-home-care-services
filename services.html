OUR SERVICES

Practical support, delivered with heart.

Flexible areas of support for older adults who want to remain comfortable and connected at home.

01

Personal Care

Respectful assistance with appropriate daily routines and personal needs, always centred on dignity and choice.

  • Daily routine support
  • Personal hygiene assistance
  • Dressing and grooming support
  • Mobility-related everyday assistance
02

Companionship

Meaningful human connection can make a major difference to everyday wellbeing.

  • Conversation and social company
  • Reading and hobbies
  • Games and activities
  • Accompaniment during everyday routines
03

Meal Support

Support with simple meal routines so the day feels organised and comfortable.

  • Meal preparation
  • Meal planning support
  • Kitchen clean-up
  • Grocery planning assistance
04

Household Help

Light practical help around the home to keep everyday spaces comfortable.

  • Light tidying
  • Laundry assistance
  • Bed-making
  • Routine household organisation
05

Errands & Appointments

Support with selected non-medical errands and appointment routines.

  • Shopping and errands
  • Appointment accompaniment
  • Routine reminders
  • Family communication as agreed
06

Respite Support

Short-term support that can give family caregivers time to work, rest or attend to other responsibilities.

  • Planned respite visits
  • Companionship while family is away
  • Routine household support
  • Flexible scheduling discussion
Important: Services listed here are examples of non-emergency home support. They do not replace emergency services, medical diagnosis or treatment. Care arrangements should be agreed based on the individual's needs and applicable local requirements.
